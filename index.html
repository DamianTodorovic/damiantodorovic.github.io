    The mechanism

    One engine between every app and your disk.

    Your assistants speak MCP. KEPTA answers, and keeps a single encrypted file. The same code path serves the apps, the HTTP API and the browser, so every client gets the same answers.

    It remembers in time

    Ask what you knew on any day.

    Facts carry validity windows. When one is replaced, the old one is marked and kept, never quietly deleted. Drag the date and watch the file tell you the truth as it stood.

    Validity
    Every note can carry valid_from and valid_to, and answers a query for a past moment.
    Supersede
    A new fact displaces the old one and points to it, so the reasoning stays traceable.
    Weighting
    Expired and replaced notes lose relevance in ranking, but never disappear from the file.

      Three ways to remember

      Three ways in, one ranked answer.

      Full text, meaning and connection are searched at once, fused into one ranking, and re-ranked on your machine. Nothing is sent away.

      Full text

      Exact words

      BM25 finds the phrase you actually used: the name, the number, the reference.

      Meaning

      Close enough

      Vector similarity finds the note that says it differently, in another language if needed.

      Connection

      Linked in

      The knowledge graph follows the links between notes, so a hit brings its neighbours.

      Fusion

      One ranking

      The three are fused with Reciprocal Rank Fusion, then re-ranked locally by term coverage, phrases, title and tags.

      Frequently asked

      01

      How do I run KEPTA?

      One command: npx kepta-mcp — the memory comes up on your machine and every MCP client (Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf) shares it. There is also a Python client (pip install kepta) and a headless HTTP API.

      02

      Is it really free?

      Yes. The engine is source-available under BUSL-1.1 and free forever — running it in your own organization is always allowed. There are no tiers inside the memory: everything that makes it smart is in the free engine.

      03

      Where do the benchmark numbers come from?

      From LongMemEval-S: 500 questions, a fixed dataset with a pinned checksum, graded by a local judge. 61.5 % — and every question, every answer and every judge verdict of every run is committed to the public repository. You can rerun the whole benchmark yourself.

      04

      What happens to my data?

      It never leaves your machine. One SQLCipher-encrypted file, keys in your OS keychain, no telemetry, no cloud, no account. Private notes never leave KEPTA over MCP.

      05

      What is KEPTA Enterprise?

      The same engine as an organization infrastructure: tenant memory for many people and agents, a tamper-proof audit chain with one-click verification, human review for agent writes, point-in-time recovery, rights and policies — SSO, MDM and air-gapped deployment included.
